Observed review

Improvement

detail · Round 2

Observed review
Side margins are narrow but both tray corners remain fully visible. Macro surface detail and lip thickness improve materially.

Improvement
Increase the tray scale by twenty percent, keep its full width with generous margins and preserve all three ivory tokens, front lettering and corners. Lower the camera slightly to emphasize the rounded raised front lip and continuous walnut grain. Reduce empty headroom, maintain sharp focus across lettering and wood, no props.

spot-2 · Round 2

Observed review
Reflection also traverses tabletop rather than exclusively rear rim, and very slight token drift persists. No shape or count changes, object movement is negligible versus distinct visible optical sweep. Background illumination spread retained as limitation.

Improvement
Lock tray and all three ivory tokens in their exact initial pixel positions. One broad soft reflection visibly travels left-to-right over exposed rear walnut rim only, revealing grain with no movement or changing grain. Constant label fill and tabletop exposure. No token drift, camera or tray motion.
