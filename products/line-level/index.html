spot-1 · Round 2

Observed review
Both block positions participate slightly rather than only the lower block, and the return is later than specified. The repaired displacement is small, continuous support is retained, and full product/label remain clear.

Improvement
Reduce lower-block displacement to five percent of width and preserve at least ninety-five percent contact support, with continuous contact shadows.

spot-2 · Round 3

Observed review
Reflection is strongest over a short middle interval rather than a uniform slow left-to-right sweep. The dark triangular patch and off-center final bubble from prior attempts are removed; glass action remains visible and clean.

Improvement
Anchor centered start/end vial and explicitly freeze bubble/liquid while confining broad diffuse reflection to the external clear glass rim.
