Observed review
The delivered hops are larger than the requested tiny ten-percent hop, but remain within the image. Moving frames soften lettering and slightly blur the silhouette. The target does not visibly shrink, so this is a bounded bounce demonstration over fixed painted rings. Settled frames retain a coherent textured sphere and readable label.

Improvement
Correction from actual round-two review: reduce motion to one tiny rigid vertical hop, only ten percent of the ball diameter, then a smaller five-percent hop. Never rise above the original top margin. No camera zoom or reframe. Entire sphere and all front lettering remain within the image throughout. Painted rings stay completely flat and fixed. Settle by second three. Keep the exact source geometry, no squash, stretch, rotation or added objects.
