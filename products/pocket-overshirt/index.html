spot-1 · Round 2 · completed

Observed: Yes. Approved: No.

Critique
Viewed twelve half-second frames. The garment remains recognizable, but the backdrop opening still arrives late. The panel lip becomes a large dark flap and adds duplicate-looking brass studs above its original corners. This creates a distracting hardware change. The final attempt should remove the failed opening mechanism.

Improvement
Begin the backdrop lip opening at half a second and finish opening at second one. Close it by second three. Keep the hanger hook, shirt collar and hem at their exact starting positions. Limit lip travel to a shallow opening only, with no panel inflation or cloth transformation. Hold the fully closed backdrop and unchanged shirt from second three through second six.
