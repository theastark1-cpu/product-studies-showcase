detail · Round 2

Observed review
The plate remains low and the spool is still clipped at the right as soft macro context. The enlarged lettering and intact seating notch improve the focal detail, but the image does not prove the divider can be removed. Outer tray edges intentionally leave this construction close-up.

Improvement
Improve observed low macro composition: lift the entire front plate toward image center and reduce empty foreground cloth to no more than one fifth of image. Keep all label plate edges fully inside broad margins, exact unchanged lettering and the same visible front divider seating notch. Preserve cross-divider geometry and cream texture. Reframe so the soft background spool is not cut by the right image edge, or naturally hidden by existing divider depth rather than clipped. No new mechanism, compartments or text.

Hold three intact objects in tidy centers.

spot-2 · Round 2

Observed review
The lift is modest and force-free, with the insert ends crossing above the outer wall edge during the raised phase. The exact seated end reference restores the bottom contact and original four-compartment layout. This is a stylized construction demonstration consistent with the macro seat, not validation of a real removable mechanism. Camera framing settles slightly and a two-second fully seated hold is not established.

Improvement
Correct observed insert drift and invented strips: the two crossing walls are permanently joined as one clean rigid cross-shaped insert, with no separate fragment. Lift only one quarter of divider wall height, maintain straight constant-width arms and intact crossing junction. No new base slots, dark holes, scattered strips or added seat geometry. Then fully lower until every divider bottom edge touches the existing tray base, exactly matching supplied seated end image. Three contents and outer tray stay fixed; front label exact.
