spot-1 · Round 3

Observed review
The delivered fold travels farther than the requested ten-percent height and one small inner crease rises into a pointed fold. This is a partial telescoping collapse and reopening, not flattening to a disc. The front panel remains readable without the prior disappearance, and the rim no longer bends sideways. The clip follows the moving rim. These samples do not establish real material mechanics or durability.

Improvement
Limit compression to ten percent height, preserve rigid full label plate and prevent deep flattening and triangular inner folds.

spot-2 · Round 2

Observed review
The stream still runs later than requested, stopping around four to four-and-a-half seconds, so a long settled hold is not established. The background curl is smaller and farther from the bowl than the prior large arch, but becomes a visible upright roll. The delivered action is a contained stylized pour with a shallow water surface, not a real capacity or leak test. Slight framing settle remains.

Improvement
Shorten pour for a clear settling interval and reduce dominant raised paper curl. Preserve contained visible water and fixed bowl.

spot-1 · Round 2 · completed

Observed: Yes. Approved: No.

Critique
The collapse is deeper than the corrected shallow one-fold instruction. The front label panel is vertically compressed and TRAVEL BOWL disappears behind the rim in the collapsed samples. Existing inner folds also form small raised triangular creases. Require a much shallower movement that preserves the full label panel and lettering.

Improvement
Correction from actual first review: the dark stone rim is a single rigid horizontal ellipse, never bending, tilting, pinching or folding sideways. Move this intact rim straight down only one existing accordion fold height, then straight up to the original expanded position. This is a shallow telescoping demonstration, not a full flattening. The orange silicone articulates solely at its existing horizontal crease. Keep the rigid front label panel unchanged and the complete attached right clip inside the frame. No inflation, diagonal crumpling, camera movement, extra bowl or new ring. End at the exact supplied source image.
