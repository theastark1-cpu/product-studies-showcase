spot-1 · Round 2 · completed

Observed: Yes. Approved: No.

Critique
Viewed twelve half-second frames. The second clip reduces the tumbling and settles the shirt earlier, but the sleeves still extend beyond the frame in the final hold. Midway, the folded shape swells into an ambiguous roll. The requested pullback did not resolve complete-product visibility. A third attempt should replace the fragile unfolding mechanism with a simpler camera move on the approved complete flat lay.

Improvement
Start unfolding within the first half-second. Keep exactly one shirt with one collar and two sleeves throughout. Unfold along two ordinary fold lines without spinning, twisting, tumbling or generating extra layers. Pull the camera back smoothly as sleeves extend, so the complete unfolded T-shirt, both sleeves and full hem fit with visible margin. Keep the swatch grid and stone background stationary. Hold the complete flat tee without motion for the final two seconds.

spot-2 · Round 2 · completed

Observed: Yes. Approved: No.

Critique
Viewed twelve half-second frames. The tee now stays front-facing and the row sequence begins earlier. But flipping transforms the thin swatches into padded pillow-like blocks with brown and black faces. This violates the requested fixed material and palette even though the final frame settles. The third attempt should use flat translation rather than flipping.

Improvement
Lock the folded shirt perfectly still and front-facing so its label remains unchanged. Begin the swatch flip at half a second. Flip the front row at 0.5 seconds, middle row at 1.5 seconds and back row at 2.5 seconds, each as simple rigid cloth cards pivoting on one edge. Both sides use only bone, pine or charcoal. Return every swatch flat by second four. Hold the settled grid and unchanged tee for the final two seconds. No shirt rotation and no tan or black swatch faces.
