spot-1 · Round 2 · rejected

Critique
Viewed twelve half-second frames. The second clip reduces the tumbling and settles the shirt earlier, but the sleeves still extend beyond the frame in the final hold. Midway, the folded shape swells into an ambiguous roll. The requested pullback did not resolve complete-product visibility. A third attempt should replace the fragile unfolding mechanism with a simpler camera move on the approved complete flat lay.

Improvement
Start unfolding within the first half-second. Keep exactly one shirt with one collar and two sleeves throughout. Unfold along two ordinary fold lines without spinning, twisting, tumbling or generating extra layers. Pull the camera back smoothly as sleeves extend, so the complete unfolded T-shirt, both sleeves and full hem fit with visible margin. Keep the swatch grid and stone background stationary. Hold the complete flat tee without motion for the final two seconds.
